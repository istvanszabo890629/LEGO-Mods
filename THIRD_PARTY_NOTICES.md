## External ASI loader

An ASI loader is a separate runtime prerequisite. Ultimate ASI Loader and its
files are not included in this repository or its release archives.
Obtain it separately from its [official project](https://github.com/ThirteenAG/Ultimate-ASI-Loader).

Game executables and game assets are not distributed with LEGO Mods.
